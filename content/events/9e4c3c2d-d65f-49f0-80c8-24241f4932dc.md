**EVENT START: Wed, 22 Jul 2026 3:30PM**

Join us at Hitchcock Nature Center this Wednesday July 22nd at 3:30 PM for a 4-mile hike through the Loess Hills of Iowa! Max elevation will be about 700ft so if you are sensitive to that please keep it in mind. The cost for this is $5 per vehicle for admission, so carpooling with some friends is definitely encouraged! Come and enjoy nature and time with other members of the community.Directions: From Interstate I-29 take the Crescent exit and travel east to The Old Lincoln Highway. Hitchcock Nature Center is located 5 miles north of Crescent off of Old Lincoln Highway.
