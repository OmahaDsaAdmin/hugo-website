_Last updated: October 2026_

### Data Collected

Phone number (voluntarily provided via the rideshare signup/request forms); the fact and timestamp of your SMS consent.

### How Data Is Used

Phone numbers are used solely to send ride-coordination SMS messages. SMS consent records are maintained for compliance.

### Third Parties

We do NOT share your phone number with third parties for marketing purposes. We use Twilio as our SMS delivery provider.

### Marketing

Your phone number will not be used for any marketing purpose.

### Retention

Phone numbers are retained as long as you have an active rideshare request or volunteer driver registration. You may request removal by emailing [info@omahadsa.org](mailto:info@omahadsa.org).

### Opt-Out

Reply **STOP** to any SMS message to immediately stop receiving further messages.

### Program Terms

See the [SMS Program Terms](/sms-terms/) for program name, description, message types, frequency, and support contact.
