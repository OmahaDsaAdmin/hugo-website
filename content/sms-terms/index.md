_Last updated: October 2026_

### Program Name

Omaha DSA Rideshare.

### Program Description

Volunteer-run community rideshare service operated by Omaha DSA. SMS notifies ride requesters and volunteer drivers of ride matching and pickup details.

### Message Types

Account notifications — ride confirmation, driver assignment, pickup reminder.

### Message Frequency

Varies with ride activity. Typically fewer than 5 messages per week per participant.

### Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply.

### Support Contact

Email [info@omahadsa.org](mailto:info@omahadsa.org) for help with the program.

### Opt-Out

Reply **STOP** to any message to opt out of further messages. Reply **START** to opt back in.

### Help

Reply **HELP** to any message for help, or email [info@omahadsa.org](mailto:info@omahadsa.org).

### Privacy

See our [SMS Program Privacy Policy](/sms-privacy/) for details on how phone numbers and SMS consent records are handled.
